Privacy Policy for Stasis Gallery
Effective Date: August 9, 2026

Stasis Gallery ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how our local media application handles your information.

1. Information Collection and Use
Stasis Gallery is a local-first application designed to manage media on your device.

Local Media & Vault Storage: The app requests permission to access your device's storage and media files strictly to display, organize, and manage your photos and videos locally, as well as handle local encrypted Secure Vault storage and trash staging on your device. We do not collect, upload, or store your media files on any external servers.

Biometric Security: Optional biometric authentication (fingerprint or face unlock) is handled locally by your device hardware and operating system. We do not access, collect, or store your biometric data.

Payments and Donations: Voluntary donation features are currently unavailable. When active in future updates, any donations will be processed securely via Google Play Billing, and we will not receive, process, or store your financial information or credit card numbers.

2. Third-Party Services
Stasis Gallery does not use third-party analytics trackers, advertising networks, or external data collection tools.

3. Changes to This Privacy Policy
We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Thus, you are advised to review this page periodically for any changes.
