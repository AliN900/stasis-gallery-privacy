Privacy Policy for Photo Gallery - Stasis Gallery

Developed by: AHB Dev
Effective Date: August 9, 2026

Stasis Gallery ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how our local media application handles your information.

1. Information Collection and Use

Stasis Gallery is a local-first application designed to manage media on your device.

Local Media & Vault Storage: The app requests permission to access your device's storage and media files strictly to display, organize, and manage your photos and videos locally, as well as handle local encrypted Secure Vault storage and trash staging on your device. We do not collect, upload, or store your media files on any external servers.

Biometric Security: Optional biometric authentication (fingerprint or face unlock) is handled locally by your device hardware and operating system. We do not access, collect, or store your biometric data.

Payments and Donations: Optional voluntary donations are available via HUAWEI In-App Purchases on HUAWEI devices. All payment processing is handled securely by HUAWEI AppGallery. We do not receive, process, or store your financial information or credit card numbers.

2. Third-Party Services

Stasis Gallery uses HUAWEI AppGallery services for optional in-app purchases and app updates. HUAWEI's privacy policy applies to those services. We do not use third-party analytics trackers, advertising networks, or external data collection tools.

3. Permissions

The app requests the following permissions:

  • Read access to photos and videos (to display and manage your media)
  • Manage media (to rename, move, or delete files on your behalf)
  • Biometric authentication (to secure your private vault)
  • Internet (only for optional in-app purchases and app updates)

You may deny these permissions. The app will continue to function with limited features.

4. Data Retention

All data is stored locally on your device. Uninstalling the app removes all local data.

5. Children's Privacy

Stasis Gallery does not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. You are advised to review this page periodically for any changes.
