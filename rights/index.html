Your Rights

Because Stasis Gallery processes all data locally on your device and never uploads it to any server, you already have full control over your data:

  • Access: All your media is accessible directly in the app.
  • Deletion: Delete any item from the gallery, trash, or vault at any time. Uninstalling the app removes all local data.
  • Portability: Export or share any file via the app's built-in share function.
  • Rectification: Rename or edit any item from the app.
  • Withdraw consent: Revoke permissions in Android Settings at any time.
